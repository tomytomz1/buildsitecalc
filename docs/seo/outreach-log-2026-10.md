## Rules / measurement

- One email per highly relevant recipient, and at most one follow-up after
  14 days if there has been no response. No mass mailing or invented
  relationships. Stop immediately on decline or opt-out.
- Check for positive replies before sending a follow-up.
- Confirm links via the institution's live article / guide.
- Goal: 5 distinct, editorially relevant referring domains. Track verified
  links by adding their published source URL and observation date here.
